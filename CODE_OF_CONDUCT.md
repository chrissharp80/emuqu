# Code of Conduct

This is a small, solo-maintained project. The standard is short because it does
not need to be long.

## Expected

Be direct and be kind. Disagree with the work, not the person. Assume the other
party is competent and acting in good faith until you have specific evidence
otherwise — and when you do have it, say so plainly rather than hinting.

Report what you observed, not what you assume it means. "This crashed on
launch, here is the log" is useful. "This is broken, obviously untested" is not.

## Not accepted

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, and unwelcome attention.
Publishing someone's private information. Deliberately derailing a discussion.

Health data is involved here. Anyone who shares another person's health
information without their consent, or uses this project to do so, is out.

## Enforcement

Reports go to chrissharp80@gmail.com and are handled privately. The maintainer
may remove comments, close threads, or block accounts. There is no appeals
process; this is one person's project.
